How each video is made

  1. 1

    Song selection. Chosen from the most voted requests.

  2. 2

    Synced lyrics. Timestamps are imported and checked line by line.

  3. 3

    Line assignment. Each line, and individual words where parts overlap, is assigned to a member.

  4. 4

    Export and publish. Video and thumbnail are exported and uploaded. Viewer corrections are applied afterward.

About Anwyl 2

Clean, accurate line distribution videos made with community input.

Anwyl 2 is an independent channel making color-coded line distribution videos, mostly western boybands. This hub lets viewers request songs, vote on what comes next, and collaborate directly on synced lyrics and member color assignments.

1. Request & Vote

Search YouTube to submit a song request or upvote tracks you want to see next.

2. Sync & Assign

Import LRC timestamps, fine-tune line timing, and color-code lines or individual words.

3. Collaborate

Suggest member parts on any project and review line-by-line community edits.
