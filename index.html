How each video is made

  1. 1

    Song selection. Chosen from the most voted requests.

  2. 2

    Synced lyrics. Timestamps are imported and checked line by line.

  3. 3

    Line assignment. Each line, and individual words where parts overlap, is assigned to a member.

  4. 4

    Export and publish. Video and thumbnail are exported and uploaded. Viewer corrections are applied afterward.
